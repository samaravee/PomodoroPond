About

Koi Pond Pomodoro Timer helps you ease between work and rest.

Breaks can turn into scrolling, which makes it harder to get back to work. Here, the break is a pond. When the work countdown ends, the koi return and the water comes alive. Click a koi, rustle the plants, move your mouse through the water. It’s calming, and it’s meant to be a little boring, so after a few minutes you’re ready to start again on your own.

When you press Start, the koi swim away and the pond settles, so nothing competes for your attention while you focus. Soft sound cues mark every transition, and you can mute them any time.
